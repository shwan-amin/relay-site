Relay privacy policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Who uses Relay

Relay is a personal tool used only by its owner. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What Google data Relay accesses

Relay signs in to a single Google account that belongs to Relay itself (not the owner's personal account), with these permissions:

  • Google Drive and Google Docs: to create folders and documents the owner asks for, and to read documents the owner names.
  • Gmail (read): to summarise email in Relay's own inbox, only when the owner asks.
  • Gmail (compose): to save reply drafts. An email is sent only after the owner reviews that exact draft and confirms it.

How the data is used and stored

Data is processed on the owner's own computer, only to do what the owner asked. The sign-in token is stored on that computer and readable only by the owner. Relay does not delete or change existing files or email.

Sharing

Relay does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising. Relay's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops Relay from reaching the account.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/shwan-amin/relay-site.
